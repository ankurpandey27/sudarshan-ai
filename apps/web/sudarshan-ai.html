    How it works
    1. 1

      Let it work here

      Tabs open in this window while Sudarshan AI searches and applies, and close when each one is done. Keep it open; it can sit behind your other windows.

    2. 2

      Log in once

      Sign in to your job sites in this window (Lakshya → Settings → Site logins opens each one). Logins stay in this browser on your computer; Sudarshan AI never sees your passwords.

    3. 3

      Step in any time

      A captcha or a stuck form keeps its tab open. Solve it there and Sudarshan AI carries on by itself, and learns your answers and the steps for next time.

    Everything stays on this computer.
