Apply while you live your life.

Sudarshan AI is a job application agent that runs on your own computer. It searches job sites, scores every job against your profile, fills and sends the applications you approve, and comes back to tell you what happened.

Why the name

In the Dwapar Yug, Shri Krishna's Sudarshan Chakra was released once and did the rest on its own: it went out, finished its task with precision, and returned to his finger. That is what this agent does. The main page is called Lakshya (लक्ष्य, "the target"), the aim Arjuna never took his eyes off. Here the target is your next job.

What makes it different

  • Everything stays on your computer. Your resume, answers and logins never leave it. There is no account and no server of ours.
  • You stay in charge. By default nothing is sent until you approve it. Sudarshan AI asks you only what it cannot know, once, and never guesses personal facts.
  • It gets faster with use. Every answer is remembered and reused, so after a few dozen applications most forms fill without any AI call.
  • Any AI, or none. Bring a key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter, run a free local model (Ollama, LM Studio), or use no AI at all.

How it works

Five steps, in a loop, all day long while the agent runs.

  1. Search. Sudarshan AI searches the job sites you switched on for your job titles and locations, every few hours. It also takes any job link you paste in, from any career site.
  2. Score. Each job is compared with your profile: your skills (and years with each), experience, salary, location and the companies or titles you never want. It gets a score from 0 to 100 and a short reason.
  3. You approve. Strong matches wait on the Review page. Approve them one by one or all at once. In Auto mode, jobs above your auto-apply score are queued for you.
  4. Apply. One job at a time, at a human pace, in its own visible browser window: it opens the job, presses Apply, fills every field from your profile and answers, uploads your resume and sends it.
  5. Report back. Every result lands on Lakshya and the Flight log. Anything that needs you (a captcha, a login, a question) appears in Needs attention and, if you like, as a desktop or Telegram alert.
Why it is fast. Sudarshan AI never lets an AI "look at a screenshot and click". It reads the whole form in one pass, answers from your profile and memory first, asks the AI at most once for whatever is left, and fills everything in one pass. A job site it has seen before is replayed from a saved recipe with no AI at all.

Install and start

About five minutes the first time. Nothing to sign up for.

You need

  • Node.js 22.13 or newer (the installer below adds it if missing).
  • Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Brave.
  • Windows, macOS or Linux. No database, Docker or Python.

One-command install

It installs Node.js if needed, downloads Sudarshan AI, adds a Sudarshan AI shortcut and starts it. Installed this way, it updates itself each time it starts.

Windows (PowerShell):

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ankurpandey27/sudarshan-ai/master/install.ps1 | iex

macOS or Linux (terminal):

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ankurpandey27/sudarshan-ai/master/install.sh | bash

Or run it yourself

git clone https://github.com/ankurpandey27/sudarshan-ai.git
cd sudarshan-ai
npm start

The first run installs, builds and downloads a small answer-matching model (about 130 MB, once), then opens http://localhost:4747. Later runs start in seconds. To stop it, press Ctrl + C in the terminal.

First-run setup

A four-step wizard. Only the resume is required.

  1. Choose a brain. Pick an AI provider, paste its key (local models need none), press Test connection, then Save model. See AI model for which one to pick.
  2. Your resume. Drop your PDF. Sudarshan AI reads it into a profile on your computer. Then fill in your notice period, current CTC and expected CTC: nearly every form asks for them.
  3. Your answers (optional). Download the Excel template, fill in what you know, upload it. See The Excel sheet.
  4. Where to look. Your job titles and locations (add Remote for remote roles), then log in to LinkedIn and Naukri in the browser window Sudarshan AI opens.
Tip for your first run: in Settings → Agent, turn on Stop before the final Submit. Sudarshan AI fills every form and waits for you to press Submit. Check a few, then turn it off.

A typical day

  1. Open Sudarshan AI and press Start agent (bottom left). It searches, scores and starts applying to anything already approved.
  2. Spend a few minutes on Review: approve the jobs you want.
  3. Answer anything on Questions. Each answer unblocks every job waiting on it.
  4. Check Needs attention now and then: tick a captcha, log in again, or fix a setting.
  5. In the evening, read the day on Lakshya (or your daily summary). Sudarshan AI rests outside your active hours and carries on the next day.
The pages

Lakshya

Your job hunt at a glance.

What it is

The main page, in four views: Mission control (live), Overview, Insights and Activity.

Why

One look tells you whether Sudarshan AI is working, what it achieved and what needs you, without reading logs.

How it works

Everything updates live while the agent runs. Overview and Insights cover 7, 30 or 90 days, for one platform or all of them.

The views

ViewWhat you see
Mission controlOpens first. The live view: a radar of every job waiting for you, what the agent is doing right now, what needs you, and today on a timeline. See Mission control.
OverviewApplications sent, Jobs found, Match rate (share of jobs worth a look) and Waiting (in the queue, to review, questions for you). The Pipeline shows how many jobs went from found, to worth a look, to approved, to applied.

Today and Apply on

On Overview, below the charts, Today shows how many applications went out today and how much of each site's daily limit is used: a bar turns orange when a site has reached its limit and waits until tomorrow. Next to it, Apply on has a card for every job site, with what Sudarshan AI does there, today's applications against the limit, and how many jobs are queued. The switch on each card turns that site on or off; a site that is off keeps its jobs waiting, nothing is lost. A card also says when a site is paused, in careful mode, or refusing applications for a while.

The controls

  • Start agent / Stop agent (bottom left, on every page). Stop lets the application in progress finish, then rests.
  • Search now runs a search straight away instead of waiting for the next scheduled one.
  • The yellow bar ("2 need you") opens Needs attention.
  • The moon button (bottom left) switches light and dark. This guide follows it.
The pages

Mission control

Lakshya's first view: watch the chakra go out, finish the task and return.

What it is

A live view of your hunt: a radar of every job waiting for you, a window on what the agent's browser is doing, what needs you, and today on a timeline - with a command bar to act in one sentence.

Why

Lists and charts tell you what happened. This shows it happening: where your best matches are, which country they are in, and each application as it flies out.

How it works

Everything is real and live: the dots are your jobs, the comets are the agent's applications, the pictures are its browser's own. Nothing moves that the agent is not doing.

The radar

Every job waiting in Review or queued to apply is a dot, coloured by its site (the 200 best matches when there are more).

Acting on a job: on a job to review, A approves, S skips and X dismisses; on a queued job, N applies now and U moves it back to review. Esc closes the card. These do exactly what the same buttons on Review do.

The command bar

Say what you want in a sentence, then press Enter. Ctrl + K jumps to it.

  • It understands: remote, hybrid, on-site; in your country ("in India", "my country") or abroad ("abroad", "not in India"); a score ("above 80", "85+"); a site ("on LinkedIn"); how recent ("today", "this week", "last 3 days"); and words to leave out ("skip anything asking Java", "without PHP or Angular"). Left-out words match whole words, so "Java" does not leave out JavaScript jobs.
  • Starting with apply, approve or queue approves the matching jobs in Review - the button shows how many first. Anything else shows them in Review with the filters Review has (work type, where, how recent).
  • It is read on your computer, without any AI - it works the same with every model, or none. Words it does not know are simply not used; the chips always show what was.

Live Eye

The application in progress: the newest picture of the agent's browser (one is taken at each step of the form) and each step as it happens - "filled 4 fields (3 profile, 1 memory, 0 AI)", "Submit application". When nothing is being applied to, it says why: resting, the time of the next application, or nothing approved yet. The pictures are the same ones kept for each attempt's replay in Applications.

Needs you

Questions only you can answer, with their choices as one-tap buttons (or a box to type in, pre-filled with a suggestion, when a question takes several choices) and how many jobs each answer unblocks; and jobs to do by hand (a captcha, a login) with Continue when their tab is still open and Open in agent browser. The count is what waits on you now: questions, and hand-overs whose tab is still open. Answers are saved to Answer memory, as on the Questions page.

Flight recorder

Every application, hand-over, search and failure from today's Flight log, on one line. Drag along it (or use the arrow keys) to see what had happened by then and the nearest event; let go and it returns to now.

Calm when you want it. With "reduce motion" turned on in your system settings, the radar and its comets stay still; everything else works the same.
The pages

Needs attention

What is stopping Sudarshan AI right now, most serious first, and how to fix each one.

What it is

A list of problems and suggestions: a question waiting, a login that ran out, a pace that is too fast, an AI key with no credit.

Why

Sudarshan AI never fails quietly. Instead of a stuck queue, you get a plain explanation and a button that takes you to the fix.

How it works

Each card says what happened and How to fix it. Orange cards block applications; blue ones are tips. Close a card with ×.

The count next to Needs attention in the sidebar shows only what needs you, not tips. See When something goes wrong for the common cards.

The pages

Review

The jobs Sudarshan AI found and scored. Approve a batch and it applies to them one by one.

What it is

Every job worth a look, with its score (0-100), company, location, platform, salary when known, and the skills it matched.

Why

You decide where you apply. Sudarshan AI does the searching and sorting so a few minutes a day is enough.

How it works

Approve jobs one by one, select several, or press Approve all N scoring X+. Approved jobs join the queue.

The tabs

TabWhat it holds
To reviewJobs scoring at or above your review score and not decided yet.
Approved (queue)Jobs waiting to be applied to, in the order Sudarshan AI will take them (Apply order): job links you added first, then, with Apply to jobs in my country first on, jobs in your country, then jobs that do not say where, then jobs abroad; within each, the best matches first. Move to review takes one out.
SkippedJobs scored too low, or excluded by your rules (a company or title word you never want, salary below your expected CTC, not remote, a junior role). The reason is shown on each; approve one anyway if you disagree.

How the score is made

  1. Your exclusions first: never-apply companies, skipped title words, remote-only, posted within.
  2. A free rule-based score: skills you have (and your years with each), experience, salary against your expected CTC, location.
  3. With AI scoring on, the AI reads the job description in batches of several jobs per call and adds its view.
  4. A job asking for one of your core skills is never skipped for a low score; it waits here, saying why.

Questions

What it is

Questions from application forms that your profile and saved answers do not cover, such as a referrer's name or a city-specific question.

Why

Sudarshan AI never makes up facts about you. When it cannot know, it asks once instead of guessing.

How it works

Answer and press Save answer. The answer goes to memory and every job waiting on it goes back into the queue.

  • High-stakes questions (salary, notice, visa, legal declarations, date of birth, ID numbers) are never guessed by the AI. They come from your profile or from you.

Your real work, in your words, for written answers.

What it is

A short interview about your proudest work, something you made faster, a hard problem, a time you led.

Why

Platforms & limits

Which job sites to use, and how many applications a day on each.

What it is

An on/off switch and a daily limit for every job site, company career sites and job links you add.

Why

Job sites restrict accounts that apply faster than a person could. Low limits protect your accounts.

How it works

Setting up Telegram

Telegram sends you the same messages as the desktop notifications - when an application needs you, and the daily summary - on your phone, wherever you are.

  1. In Telegram, open @BotFather, send /newbot and pick any name. It replies with a token like 123456:AAH....
  2. Paste the token into the box and press Save. The card says which bot it saved.
  3. Send your new bot any message (like "hi"), then press Connect. The card shows Connected; Send today's summary now above it sends a test.

The token is stored encrypted on your computer. Only you and your bot are in the chat. Disconnect removes it.

Setting up replies from your email

Sudarshan AI reads your mailbox every 30 minutes and matches replies to your applications - "received", a test, an interview, an offer, or "not selected" - shown as a badge on each application in Applications.

  1. Make an app password for your mailbox - not your normal password. Gmail: Google Account → Security → 2-Step Verification → App passwords. Yahoo: Account security → Generate app password. iCloud: appleid.apple.com → Sign-In and Security → App-Specific Passwords.
  2. Enter your email address and the app password, then press Connect mailbox. Gmail, Yahoo and iCloud are known; for another provider an IMAP server box appears - enter its address (for example imap.example.com).
  3. The card shows Reading your address, when it last checked, and how many replies it has matched. Check now reads it straight away; Disconnect stops it.

It is read on your computer only, never by an AI. Only replies about your applications are kept - the sender, the subject and what they mean, never the email itself. The app password is stored encrypted, and is never in a backup.

How it works

Press Log in. A window opens on the site; sign in as usual. The row then shows Connected.

For a site whose login Sudarshan AI does not know yet (Himalayas, for example), it learns the login the first time you sign in, then shows Connected from then on. When a login runs out, Needs attention tells you and that site waits until you log in again.

Data & backups

Your data, the spreadsheet, and backups you can restore.

What it is

Spreadsheet import and export, daily backups, an off-computer backup folder, and restore.

Why

Everything Sudarshan AI learned about you lives in one file. A backup means a damaged disk never costs you more than a day.

How it works

A full backup is made once a day by itself. Point Also copy each backup to this folder at a synced folder to keep a copy elsewhere.

Where your data is

ComputerData folder
WindowsC:\Users\<you>\.sudarshan (or .job-apply-agent from before the rename)
macOS/Users/<you>/.sudarshan
Linux/home/<you>/.sudarshan

Inside: agent.db (profile, answers, jobs, applications, what it learned), backups (the last 7 daily backups), secret.key (unlocks your saved AI keys), browser-profile (the agent's logins), uploads (your resumes), screenshots (kept 30 days) and logs (kept 14 days).

Old files are cleared when Sudarshan AI starts and then every 24 hours while it runs - there is no fixed time of day; while it is closed nothing runs, and the next start catches up. The same pass makes the daily backup. An attempt keeps its record (steps, fields, result) after its pictures are cleared.

Restoring a backup

  1. Open Data & backups → Restore from a backup and choose the file (a daily agent-YYYY-MM-DD.db or a downloaded sudarshan-backup-....db).
  2. Press Restore. Your current data is kept as before-restore-....db, Sudarshan AI restarts by itself, and the page reloads.
  3. Enter your AI key again: keys are never in a backup, so a backup can never leak them.
Backups hold your personal details. Keep them private; never share or upload them publicly.
Under the hood

How questions are answered

Your facts first, the AI last, and you whenever it cannot know.

  1. Your profile. Name, email, phone, notice period, salary, education years, links, skills with years: filled by rules, in many languages and wordings.
  2. Answer memory. The same question answered before, on any site.
  3. The AI, once per form, for whatever is left. It sees your profile, your resume, your work history and the stories that fit, and answers only what it can back up from them.
  4. You, on the Questions page, for anything it cannot know.

Rules it always keeps

  • High-stakes questions are never guessed: salary, notice period, joining date, relocation, visa and work permits, legal or signed declarations, ID or bank numbers, date of birth.
  • A pre-filled answer that contradicts your profile is corrected, for plain facts like your school, city, phone, salary and notice period. Some job sites pre-fill from an old profile of theirs.
  • Answers fit the box: "30" in a box that wants a number, not "30 days"; your phone with your country code when the box shows a dialling code. When a site turns down a "Yes" or "No" because its box takes only a number, the answer goes in again as the number the question means: your notice period in days for a question about notice or starting, the number the question names ("within 30 days"), or else 1 for Yes and 0 for No.
  • Job posts cannot steer the AI. Anything written by strangers is marked as data, never instructions. A post that tries to talk to AI tools is scored by rules only.
  • Your personal identifiers never reach the AI: phone, email, address, date of birth, PAN, Aadhaar, passport and bank details are hidden from it and filled from your profile only.

Captchas and hand-overs

What Sudarshan AI leaves to you, on purpose, and how to finish it quickly.

What

Captchas, sites that need an account or a code sent to your email or phone, and pages it cannot work out.

Why

Captchas exist to keep automated tools out. Sudarshan AI never solves or gets around them, and never creates accounts for you.

How

It fills everything else, keeps the tab open, tells you, and carries on with other jobs meanwhile.

  1. Sudarshan AI fills the whole form and stops at the captcha. The job shows Do by hand: "Filled - only the captcha is left".
  2. The tab stays open in the agent's browser, and the agent moves on to the next job.
  3. Switch to that tab and solve the captcha. Press Submit yourself, or just leave the tab: a few seconds later Sudarshan AI presses Submit for you (or press Continue in Applications).
  4. When the site confirms, the job turns Applied by itself ("Finished by you"), and anything you typed is remembered.
Fewer captchas: keep a safe pace, stay logged in to each site, and keep the agent's browser visible. Most captchas come from applying too fast or from logged-out visits.

Sudarshan AI also protects you on the way: it never fills a page reached through an advert, checks that a form is about the job before filling it, and treats a job page that shows a different job, a "404" or "no longer accepting applications" as closed.

Under the hood

Safety and privacy

  • Everything stays on your computer, in your data folder: the database, your resume, the agent's browser profile, logs, screenshots and backups.
  • API keys are encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key made on your computer, and never sent back to the page.
  • No passwords stored. You log in to job sites in the agent's own browser window.
  • Local only. The app listens on 127.0.0.1 and refuses requests from other websites.
  • What leaves your computer: traffic to the job sites you use and the AI provider you chose (none with a local model), and, only if you turn them on, Telegram (your own bot) and your mailbox (read only).
Your responsibility. Sudarshan AI acts in your own browser, on your own accounts. Automated applying may break a site's terms, and sites can restrict accounts that apply too fast. The defaults are careful, but how you use it is up to you.

Help

Questions people ask

Does it apply without asking me?

Only in Auto mode, and only to jobs at or above your auto-apply score. In Review mode (the default) nothing is sent until you approve it.

Will it lie on my applications?

No. Facts come from your profile and your own answers. The AI only answers what your profile and resume back up, never guesses high-stakes facts, and anything it cannot know comes to you.

Remote jobs abroad keep using up my limit before jobs in my country. What can I do?

Keep Settings → Agent → Apply to jobs in my country first on (it is on by default): jobs in your country go first. To approve only those, filter Review by Where: In <your country> and press Approve all. Check that your Profile has your country.

Can it solve captchas?

No, by design. It fills everything else and hands the captcha to you; after you tick it, Sudarshan AI finishes the application.

How much does the AI cost?

Very little, or nothing with a free tier or local model. Memory answers most questions, and the daily token budget caps what a paid model can use.

Can I use it on two computers?

Yes: back up on one, restore on the other (Settings → Data & backups), then enter your AI key again.

How do I update or remove it?

Installed with the one-line installer, it updates itself on start. From a clone: git pull then npm start. To remove it, delete the sudarshan-ai folder and your data folder.
